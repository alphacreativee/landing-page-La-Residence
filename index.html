Welcome to La Résidence

La Résidence is a premium serviced apartment offering for modern cosmopolitan living.
Blending the comfort of home with the attentive service of a luxury hotel, it provides thoughtfully designed living spaces for global professionals and long-stay guests. Operated by Shilla Hotel, the world leading luxury hotel brand, La Résidence offers the warmth of home together with world-class hospitality. Residents enjoy five-star services, premium amenities and personalized care for a seamless everyday living experience.
